Welcome to the wiki for the world of Three Skies!

Once beholden to capricious petty gods, tyrants and superstitions, the world is entering a new age, and it will never be the same again.

For decades, the continent has enjoyed an era of unprecedented peace and prosperity under the rule of the *Itolkanwa*, or League of Three Skies. The world has been united by their True Faith, made safe by their brave riparines, and brought closer by their canals. All amid a tenuous truce with their enemies.

But good things never last. 

The same faith that helped unite the League now calls for war, convinced that apocalypse will come should the pagans in the north are not brought to heel. 

Beneath it all, a shadow war rages between higher beings.
